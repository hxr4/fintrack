# Phase 3 manual test on the phone

This install was a fresh one, so start with onboarding.

## 0. Onboarding
Onboard with real values, as in phase 2. Add one supply item with an honest "last bought" date (for example soap, 90, 45 days). Skip recurring costs for now.

## 1. Supplies
1. Plan > Supplies and bike > Supplies. Soap shows days left, "₹X saved of ₹90" and a bar.
2. Tap Soap > Restocked, keep the amount, and save. You see "Restock of Soap logged", the days left reset to the full lifespan, and saved goes to ₹0. The Essentials stock card on Home goes down. If it would go below zero, Buffer covers the gap (shown under Buffer > Moves).
3. Add a second item, then edit and delete it.

## 2. Bike
1. Bike tab > Service details. Enter your interval (for example 3000 km or 4 months), the usual cost, and the last service km and date. Save. "Next service: in about N days" appears.
2. Log fuel: amount, price per litre and odometer, with "Filled to full" on. The fill appears under Recent fills with litres, and Petrol on Home goes down.
3. Log a second full-tank fill with a higher odometer. Mileage in km per litre and cost per km appear. After a few fills, the petrol suggestion line appears next to your petrol floor.
4. Try an odometer lower than the last fill. The form refuses it.
5. Set the insurance and PUC expiry dates. Each shows days left.
6. In Report > All entries, delete the fuel entry. The fill disappears from Recent fills. Undo brings both back.

## 3. Recurring
1. Recurring tab > Add recurring cost (for example a subscription, 119, every 1 month). The line shows how much is set aside each cycle. That starts from the next cycle.
2. Tap it > Paid. Recurring fund goes negative and Buffer covers it.

## 4. Balance check
1. Plan > Balance check. Enter your real bank balance and cash.
2. Compare. You see the expected and actual amounts and one neutral sentence. If they differ, three options appear.
3. Pick "Log it as unlogged spending" and Apply. You see "Plan updated from your real balance". Home updates, and the next Compare with the same numbers says "Everything matches."
4. Try "Leave it as it is" on a later check. Nothing changes.

## 5. Budget values
1. Plan > Budget values. Change the food floor and save. The Food card health and refill amounts follow the new floor.
2. Change the split to something that does not add up to 100. Save does nothing and the total turns amber.

## 6. Restart
Swipe the app away, reopen it and unlock. The supplies, fills, service details and balances are all still there.
